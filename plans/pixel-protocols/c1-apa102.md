# Batch c1-apa102: APA102-style clocked parts and the 16-bit NS108 family

Evidence, quotes and URLs: [c1-apa102.yaml](c1-apa102.yaml). Researched 2026-11-20.
Page numbers are PDF pages. "Measured" means an independent analysis (cpldcpu, PJRC),
not a maker sheet.

## Answers

- **Same frame on the wire as the APA102:** APA102, APA102C, APA107, SK9822, HD107S,
  NS107S, GE8822, LC8823 and LC8823-2020. Every one of these sheets prints the same
  frame: 32 zero bits, then 32 bits per LED (`111`, a 5-bit brightness field, then
  blue, green, red, 8 bits each, MSB first). Data is sampled on the rising clock edge,
  and the sheets print an end frame of 32 one bits. Only the printed maximum clock
  differs (table below).
- **SK9822-A:** the same 32-bit frame shape, but its sheet draws the channels
  **Green | Red | Blue** ("GRB order output light"). A transmitter needs a different
  colour order setting for it. That is a wire difference, though not a different protocol.
- **Only the brightness-to-light conversion differs (not a wire difference).** The
  APA102's 5-bit field is a ~440 Hz PWM laid over its ~19 kHz PWM (measured), while
  the SK9822 changes the LED current (measured). The other sheets copy either APA's
  "constant current output value" text or Opsco's "simultaneous control of
  OUTR\OUTG\OUTB three port current", and nobody has measured them. PWM rates
  (1.2-27 kHz as printed) also differ without touching the wire.
- **Same bits, different latch:** the SK9822 shows a frame only on the first clock
  after the _next_ 32-zero start frame. The APA102 shows each LED's data as soon as its
  frame arrives (both measured by cpldcpu). The Opsco datasheet is silent on this. No
  document or measurement covers the latch rule of APA107, HD107S, NS107S, GE8822,
  LC8823, LC8823-2020 or SK9822-A. One stream drives both kinds (cpldcpu): 32×0 start
  frame, LED frames, 32×0 "reset frame", then at least n/2 zero bits.
- **The end frame is just clock edges.** Each APA102 passes data on half a clock
  late, but the clock itself is not delayed. A string of n LEDs therefore needs at
  least n/2 extra clock edges after the last frame, and the printed 32 bits only cover
  64 LEDs (cpldcpu). For an APA102 the extra bits can be ones or zeros; the SK9822
  latch needs zeros.
- **The 16-bit parts use a different protocol:**
  - Start: at least 128 zero bits.
  - Each LED: 64 bits, which are a `1`, 15 gain bits (5 per colour) and three 16-bit values.
  - End: one extra DCLK per chip with DIN held high, after which "the new data …
    comes into effect".
  - NS108, NS108E and SK9826 print **word-for-word the same protocol pages**. They
    differ only in max clock (40 vs 30 MHz) and PWM (27 vs 4 kHz).
  - Rose Lighting's HD108 V1.1.0 sheet disagrees with the IC maker on two points.
    Its colour order is R G B where Newstar/Opsco draw G R B, and it says "the end frame
    is not required".
- **APA102 32-bit mode on the 16-bit parts:** no maker document describes one. Only
  Rose's superseded 2019 HD108 sheet claims "fully compatible with APA102". Its frame
  text is copied from the HD107S, and its drawing is the HD107S drawing with "16bits"
  painted over the labels. Treat the claim as untested.
- **Minimum clock or timeout:** none is documented anywhere.
  - APA's APA102C sheet recommends FCLK "0-5" MHz.
  - The LC8823-2020 sheet prints FCLK min 0.
  - cpldcpu: "I have no recommendation for a maximum or minimum SPI clock speed."
  - No sheet mentions a timeout or a reset by idle time.

## Frames bit by bit (first bit on the wire at the left)

APA102 family (every 8-bit part here except SK9822-A):

```
start  00000000 00000000 00000000 00000000          32 x 0 (more zeros are harmless)
LED    1 1 1 g4 g3 g2 g1 g0 | B7..B0 | G7..G0 | R7..R0  32 bits; only the first 1 matters (measured)
end    11111111 11111111 11111111 11111111          as printed; really >= n/2 clock edges
```

- SK9822-A: `1 1 1 g4..g0 | G7..G0 | R7..R0 | B7..B0`.
- SK9822 (measured): append `00000000 x4` after the last LED to latch.
- LC8823-2020 prints the header as a "Marker position" field ("It is marked as "1"")
  with no width given, then D4..D0. 32 bits total implies 3 marker bits.

16-bit, as Newstar/Opsco print it (NS108, NS108E, SK9826):

```
start  >= 128 x 0
LED    1 | 5 gain bits x3 (text: red, green, blue, S4 first) | G15..G0 | R15..R0 | B15..B0   64 bits
end    n extra DCLK with DIN high ("512 chips in cascade, need to send additional 512 DCLK")
```

HD108 per Rose V1.1.0:

```
start  >= 128 x 0
LED    1 | R gain 5 | G gain 5 | B gain 5 | R15..R0 | G15..G0 | B15..B0
end    "the end frame is not required"
```

What libraries send for the HD108 (not maker sources):

- FastLED: 64 zero bits ("64 bits works reliably"), header `[1][RRRRR][GG] [GGG][BBBBB]`,
  colour order defaulting to GRB, then (n/2)+4 bytes of 0xFF.
- braydenm's sketch: 128 zero bits, then R, G, B, then at least n one bits.

Edges and delays:

- **Sampling edge:** the rising edge, in every sheet that says, by arrows or in words.
- **Shift edge:** no maker gives one.
- **APA102 (measured):** SDO changes on the incoming rising edge. CKO is CKI inverted,
  so data reaches the next LED half a clock later and the clock itself is not delayed
  (cpldcpu). At 24 MHz there is about 15 ns from clock to data, and each LED shortens
  the clock low time it passes on, which is why long strings fail at 24 MHz (PJRC).
- **Propagation delays (TPD/TCO):** <15 ns in APA's APA102C sheet, <12 ns in the
  LC8823-2020 and Superlighting APA102-2020 sheets.

## Disagreements between documents

- **APA102 brightness:** every APA-lineage sheet says the field sets "Driving
  Current", but cpldcpu measured a ~440 Hz PWM. Advatek also says "current control".
- **Same IC, different max clock:** HD107S 40 MHz (Rose) vs NS107S 30 MHz (Newstar).
- **Resellers vs makers on clock:** Rose's comparison chart gives "APA102 … 20MHZ",
  "SK9822 … 2MHZ", "APA107 … 30MHZ" and calls these "PWM frequency". The makers print
  30 MHz (APA102C), 30 MHz (SK9822) and nothing (APA107).
- **Clock widths vs rated clock:** TCLKH > 30 ns plus TCLKL > 30 ns caps the clock
  at 16.7 MHz. That sits under the 30 MHz rating (APA102C, SK9822, GE8822, NS107S),
  the 40 MHz rating (HD107S) and the 20 MHz rating (LC8823-2020). The APA102-2020
  (15 MHz) and SK9822-A (17 ns typ) are consistent.
- **LC8823:**
  - "maximum frequency of 27KHZ serial data input" (LC8823 and LC8823-2020 covers).
  - LC8823 FPWM is "2.7" KHZ, while its product page says 27 kHz.
- **"The speed of data transmission fDIN" row:**
  - 4.15 KHZ in SK9826 and NS108E.
  - 27.15 KHZ in Newstar's 2022 NS108 upload.
  - 40 MHZ in the NS108 distributor copy.
  - The overview text gives 30/40 MHz throughout.
- **16-bit gain order:** the text says red, green, blue, but the table header right
  below it reads Green, Red, Blue (Newstar/Opsco). Rose and FastLED use R, G, B.
- **16-bit colour order, end and start:**
  - Colour order: Newstar/Opsco G R B, Rose R G B, FastLED GRB by default, braydenm R G B.
  - End: Newstar/Opsco need n extra DCLK, Rose needs none, FastLED sends (n/2)+4 bytes.
  - Start: every document says at least 128 zero bits, but FastLED uses 64.
- **Rose's behaviour text is copied:** Rose's HD107S pp. 6-7 (and its HD108 sheets)
  are cpldcpu's 2014 APA102 findings nearly word for word. They are not evidence about
  how those parts behave.
- **Controller makers:** Advatek and ENTTEC give the APA102 "PWM Rate 1920Hz". The
  APA102-2020 sheet prints 20 kHz, and cpldcpu measured ~19 kHz.
- **"Global clock latching mode":** APA's 2018 APA102C sheet says "Adopt special global
  clock latching mode to make the picture more consistent", which nobody has explained
  or measured.

## Who copied whom (useful for the "same thing, different name" claims)

- **Shiji/iPixel APA102C (2014)** was copied by:
  - APA Electronic's 2018 APA102C pp. 1-3 (FOSC changed to 5.1 MHz);
  - Newstar's APA107;
  - Newstar's NS107S pp. 2-4 and 6;
  - Superlighting's APA102-2020 copy;
  - an unbranded retype hosted by Signlite.
- **Opsco SK9822 Rev 01 (2016)** was copied by:
  - Greeled's GE8822 (name swapped);
  - LEDYi's "SK9822 RGB" Rev 02 (Andy Zhu, 2018-07-09);
  - LED Color's LC8823. It is the same file and revision record as LEDYi's, with
    "30MHZ" changed to "27KHZ" and FPWM 1.2 changed to 2.7.
  - Newstar's NS107S p. 5;
  - Opsco's own SK9822-A, with the order changed to GRB.
- **APA Electronic's APA102C protocol page** reappears in LC8823-2020 in English. The
  LC8823-2020 PDF has the same author ("Robert.lo") as the Normand-lettered APA-102-2020
  sheet.
- **cpldcpu's 2014 article** reappears in Rose's HD107S V1.0.1 pp. 6-7, then in Rose's
  HD108 V1.0.1, then in Rose's HD108 V1.1.0 (adapted to 64 bits).
- **Opsco SK9826 REV04 (2019)** was copied by:
  - Newstar's SK9826 (company name swapped);
  - Newstar's NS108E;
  - Newstar's NS108.
    All three still title the optical table "SK9826".

## Database corrections (not applied)

1. **`pixels/sk9826` creator:** the entry says Newstar LED Co., but the sheet is
   Opsco's (SPC/SK9826 REV04, "DONGGUAN OPSCO OPTOELECTRONICS CO., LTD",
   <https://ecksteinimg.de/Datasheet/LED/LED0032/SK9826_REV04_EN.pdf>). The entry's
   Newstar PDF is the same sheet with the company name swapped.
2. **`pixels/sk9826` and `pixels/ns108e`, `color.order: RGB`:** both sheets draw
   b47-b32 OUTG, b31-b16 OUTR, b15-b0 OUTB and say "GRB order to light" (p. 7).
3. **`pixels/hd108`, `color.order: RGB`:** this matches Rose V1.1.0 but contradicts the
   IC maker's NS108 sheet (GRB). Mark it disputed.
4. **`pixels/sk9822-a`:** add `color.order: GRB`, since the frame is drawn Green | Red |
   Blue (p. 6), unlike the SK9822's B G R.
5. **`pixels/apa107`:**
   - `data.bitrate: 30MHz` is not in Newstar's sheet, which prints no clock rating.
     The only 30 MHz figure is Rose's (a reseller of the successor part).
   - The 9 kHz PWM, which the entry calls unsourced, does appear in Rose's HD107S sheet
     (pp. 3, 7), but only as Rose's claim.
6. **`pixels/lc8823`, `pwm_frequency: 27kHz`:** the datasheet table says FPWM 2.7 KHZ
   (p. 4); only the product page says 27 kHz. No clock rating exists for it.
7. **`pixels/lc2823-2020`:**
   - The ID and file name say "lc2823", but the part is the LC8823-2020.
   - `pwm_frequency: 27kHz` has no support in its datasheet, which prints no PWM figure.
   - A bitrate could be added: 20 MHz max (typ 15 MHz), p. 2-3.
8. **`pixels/apa102`, `data.bitrate: 30MHz`:**
   - Both 2020-package sheets say 15 MHz.
   - The 5050's only rating is APA's APA102C sheet: 30 MHz limit, 0-5 MHz recommended.
   - The entry's own datasheet (Shiji) prints none.
9. **`pixels/apa102c`, `datasheet.url`:** this is the Shiji/iPixel sheet. APA
   Electronic's own APA102C sheet (<https://www.lcd-module.de/eng/pdf/zubehoer/APA102C.pdf>,
   REV.01 2018-10-11) is already cited in `sources.data.type` and is the better
   `datasheet.url`.
10. **`pixels/sk9822` creator "Opsco/Newstar":** only an Opsco SK9822 sheet was found. Minor.

## Still open

- **Latch rule** of APA107, HD107S, NS107S, GE8822, LC8823, LC8823-2020 and SK9822-A:
  do they behave like the APA102 or like the SK9822? To test, send one frame with
  only a ones end frame and see whether it appears.
- **APA102-2020 latch:** whether the Normand-sheet APA102-2020 latches like an SK9822.
  cpldcpu noted in 2016 that APA's "APA102-2C" "seem to be the SK9822".
- **HD108 on real parts:** the colour order, whether end clocks are needed (and how
  many), and whether 64 zero bits are always enough to start a frame.
- **Clock output:** whether it is inverted or delayed, for every part except the
  APA102. The NS108's one-DCLK-per-chip end rule hints at a whole-clock delay per chip,
  but no document says so.
- Newstar's second NS107S PDF (`product/20190710102128174.pdf`) is archived truncated
  at 1 MiB and could not be read. Newstar's 2022 NS108 upload is truncated the same way
  (17 pages recovered); the distributor copy is complete.
