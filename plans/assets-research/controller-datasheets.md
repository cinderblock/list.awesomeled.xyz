## Things to double-check

1. **Advatek Mk2 user manuals (7) and all Advatek URLs** are content-hashed `cdn.sanity.io` files. They change whenever Advatek re-uploads, so they will rot. The mirror protects us.
2. **Non-`.pdf` endpoints:** spinode (VP download API, which also bumps their download counter) and both DiGidot entries (Odoo `/document/<n>`). Both serve real PDFs and pass `--check`.
3. **reactivo-2:** the URL was inferred from LSS's shop upload path. It isn't linked on a page I could see.
4. **Borderline manual calls:**
   - Included: rpi-28d (spec list, not a table) and replay (2-page install sheet).
   - Excluded: SP608E (features list, no table) and PixNet 4 (no spec section).
5. **Falcon:** every manual is a public Google Doc, and `…/export?format=pdf` returns a PDF (F16V5 is 15 MB, F48 is 9.6 MB). I skipped these as not a stable maker PDF and not a datasheet. They're easy to add if you want them.
6. **Languages:** the Electroconcept manuals are French, the Diamex ones German, and Lumikit's Portuguese.
7. **Possible data issue, spi-matrix:** the current LSS tech sheet says 7 universes/output (28 total) and "Maximal Current 10A", but this worktree's entry has `power.current.max: 20`. Master already carries a provenance note on that value, so another workstream likely owns it.
