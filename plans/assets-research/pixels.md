## Suspected data errors / follow-ups (not changed)

- **apa102** (outside this batch): the image source, Adafruit product 2343, is now titled "DotStar … [SK9822]", so the photo may show an SK9822. Its `third_party_testing` points at neon-world id=167, which is the APA-109-RGBW product page.
- **ns108e, sk9826**: the `dead_links` items mark PDFs that serve fine (200 and a valid PDF on 2026-11-18). The link-check owner should re-verify them.
- **sk9822-a**: `creator: Newstar LED Co.` is probably wrong. Normand publishes the datasheet and sells the product.
- **ws2816**: the entry says `brightness_bits: 8+5` with 5-bit per-channel brightness. WorldSemi's WS2816B datasheets describe 16-bit grayscale plus internal 4-bit gamma (20-bit effect) and no 5-bit per-channel brightness. WorldSemi now names the family WS2916.
- **ns107s / ns108 vs hd107s / hd108**: Newstar sells them as "NS107S HD107S" and "NS108 HD108". Its NS108 datasheet calls Newstar "the original IC developer of NS107S NS108 series". These may be the same parts as Rose Lighting's HD107S/HD108.
- **pl9823**: `creator: /`. The datasheet header names BaiCheng.
- **lc8808b**: the maker names it LC8808 (no "B").
- **apa105, apa107, ns8808**: the old `datasheet.url` values were live Newstar product pages. apa107 and ns8808 are useful for `links`; apa105's is a strip page.
- **ws2813, ws2815**: the primary datasheets are now the current WorldSemi revisions, while the entry facts come from the 2016/2018 docs (kept as `sources`).
