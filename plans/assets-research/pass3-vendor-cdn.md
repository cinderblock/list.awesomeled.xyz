# Pass 3: vendor-CDN datasheets → maker copies

Done 2026-11-27 4:40 PM PST. 20 entries whose `datasheet.url` was on Adafruit, SparkFun, Pololu, RS, DigiKey, Farnell or
linux-sunxi. 12 switched, 8 unchanged. Every switched entry passes `bun run mirror-datasheets --check --only <entry>`;
`bun run validate` is 609/609 valid. Nothing committed.

Maker sites, for the next pass:

- **Worldsemi**: the redesigned site lists its current PDFs in `/datasheet-availability.js`
  (`/downloads/datasheets/en/<model>-<ver>.pdf`). The old `/web/userfiles/productfile/…` files still answer at
  `https://www.world-semi.com/…` (the bare `world-semi.com` host 301s to a `//` path).
- **APA Electronic** (neon-world.com): each product page has a "PDF document Download" link to
  `/uploads/soft/<date>/<n>.pdf`. The site is very slow (one page fetch took over 2 min), so each switched APA entry
  also gets a byte-identical Wayback copy as a `host: manufacturer` source.
- **Opsco** (opscoled.com, 欧思科): its download centre sits behind a name, phone and company form, and posts the
  choice before giving out a file. There's no direct URL to link. **Shenzhen Normand** (normandled.com) publishes
  the SK-series sheets openly at `/upload/<yyyymm>/<name>.pdf` (12 paged download lists, 136 PDFs). Plain `http`
  301s to `https`.
- **Greeled** (gree-leds.com): it's an LED-strip vendor that hosts other makers' IC sheets (FW1906, SM16703, UCS2904,
  APA102-2020). Neither its live download page nor any of its 54 PDFs captured by Wayback is an LPD sheet.
- **Molex**: molex.com times out for every automated client (curl and WebFetch), but Wayback has Molex's own file.
- **Chogori Technology** (chogori-tech.com; chogori.cn didn't connect): its Support page offers only four brochures,
  behind an email subscription form. No series datasheets.
- **Orange Pi**: each board's support page has a "Datasheet" link to a Google Drive file. The PC Plus one is
  `Allwinner_H3_Datasheet_V1.2.pdf`, byte-identical to the current URL's file (MD5 6643fe4e…). The Zero LTS one
  returned Drive's "Quota exceeded" page.
- The Internet Archive went offline several times during the run (Wayback, CDX and the availability API).

## Suspected data errors

- **pixels/apa102**: the old datasheet was an APA102C sheet (iPixel). Any field read from it describes the APA102C.
  `third_party_testing` (neon-world `product_detail.php?cid=59&id=167`) is APA's **APA-109-RGBW** product page,
  not testing of the APA102.
- **pixels/apa102, apa102c**: APA's own sheets give 18mA per channel (entries: `channel_current: 22mA`) and "PWM: 20KHz"
  (apa102c: `pwm_frequency: 19.2kHz`). They also give a 30MHz maximum clock but a 0–5MHz recommended range.
  APA-102C-NEW says the LED is off at power-on.
- **pixel-ics/ws2811**: the maker's V1.52 sheet says OUTR/G/B and DOUT withstand 20V (entry: `output_voltage: 12V`),
  DIN withstands 9.5V, PWM is 4kHz (entry: `pwm_frequency: 2kHz`), packages are SOP8 and MSOP8 only (entry also lists
  FSOP-8 and DIP-8), and signal reach is 5m (the original sheet says 10m). The original sheet (now a source) gives 400Hz
  and 12V. Spec fields not edited.
- **pixels/ws2812**: `pin_count: 4`, but the WS2812/WS2812S in both sheets is a 6-pin 5050 part (DOUT, DIN, VCC, NC,
  VDD, VSS). The 4-pin values (packages 3535/2040/2020, the LCSC price for WS2812B-B/W) seem to come from WS2812B.
  WS2812S V1.4 also gives VDD/VCC 3.7–5.3V and R channel current 16.5mA (entry: `channel_current: 18.5mA`), and
  reaches 3m between pixels (the original says 5m).
- **pixels/sk9822**: Normand rev 07 shows FPWM 4.6kHz (the table is garbled in text extraction), while the entry says
  `pwm_frequency: 1.2kHz` (Pololu rev 01). Rev 07 also gives VDD +3.7–5.5V; rev 01 gives an absolute maximum of
  −0.5–+5.5V.
- **pixel-ics/lpd6803, lpd8803, lpd8806**: `creator: Greeled Electronic` is doubtful. Greeled is a strip vendor that
  rebrands other makers' sheets, and the LPD6803 sheet names no company.
- **pixels/apa106**: no `creator`. The only sheet is iPixel/Shiji-branded, and APA Electronic doesn't list the part.
