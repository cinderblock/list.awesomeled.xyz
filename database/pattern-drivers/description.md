Pattern driver software creates and sends the pixel data that makes your LEDs come alive.
From simple color cycling to complex synchronized shows, these applications are where the creative magic happens.

Options range from free open-source tools to professional-grade commercial software, and the Kind column sorts them by what they are for:

- **Sequencer** - Program a show ahead of time on a timeline, often synced to music
- **Live / VJ** - Perform or generate it in real time: VJ software, media servers, lighting consoles, creative coding
- **Player** - Play back shows made elsewhere, standalone or on a schedule (including recorder boxes)
- **Music-reactive** - Built mainly to turn audio into light
- **On-controller** - Runs on the pixel controller itself
